Oman: company setup, or setup with a 2-year investor visa

30 Oct 2026 · 3 min read

An Omani company lets you trade in the Gulf. An investor visa lets you live in Oman as its investor. Here's how our two Oman packages differ, what each includes, and what the authorities decide.

Oman is a practical base for trading, import and export, and entry into Gulf markets. Foreign investors can own an Omani LLC 100% for most activities, so you don't need a local partner to hold shares in many cases.

When clients ask us about Oman, they usually want one of two things: a company to trade through, or a company plus the right to live in Oman as its investor. We offer both as separate packages. This guide explains the difference and what each one covers.

Option 1: company setup only

This suits you if you want an Omani company to sign contracts, invoice clients and trade in the region, but you don't plan to live in Oman. Company setup starts from RM 3,500 and includes:

  • Commercial registration
  • Activity licences
  • Company documents
  • Chamber of commerce registration

Option 2: setup with a 2-year investor visa

This suits you if you also want to reside in Oman as the investor in your company. Setup with an investor visa starts from RM 7,500. It includes everything in company setup, plus:

  • An investor residence visa, valid for 2 years
  • Bank account support

The visa comes from a separate application to the Omani authorities, made after the company exists. The authorities review it and decide. We prepare the paperwork carefully, but no service can guarantee a visa.

Company and visa are two different things

Forming a company in Oman, or anywhere else, does not by itself give you a visa or residency. A company is a legal entity that can trade. A residence visa is a permission for a person, granted by the government. That's why we list them as two packages, and why the visa package costs more: it covers the extra application and coordination.

How to choose

  • You run the business from abroad. Company setup is usually enough. You can trade through the company without living in Oman.
  • You plan to spend time in Oman and manage the business there. Look at setup with an investor visa.
  • You aren't sure yet. Tell us what the company will do and where you will be based. We'll explain which route fits before you pay anything.

Points to check before you start

  • Your activity. Foreign ownership is open for many activities, but some remain restricted. We check your activity first.
  • Office space. Some activities need a physical office. When yours does, office space is quoted separately.
  • Bank account. The bank decides whether to open an account after its own checks. We support the application; approval is the bank's decision.
  • Tax. For questions about tax on your company or personal income, speak to a qualified tax adviser.
