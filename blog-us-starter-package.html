What RM 1,199 gets you: the US Starter package explained

14 Oct 2026 · 3 min read

Our US Starter package forms your LLC and gets your EIN for one price, with the state filing fee included. Here is exactly what is inside, what is not, and when to choose a bigger package.

Many founders outside the US ask the same first question: what is the simplest way to get a real US company? For most of them, the answer is our US Starter package. It costs RM 1,199 (USD 289) and covers the essentials to form a US LLC and get its tax ID, fully remotely.

This guide explains what the package includes, what it does not, and how to tell whether it is enough for your plans.

What is included

  • State filing fee. The government fee for forming your LLC is part of the price, so there is no separate state bill at the start.
  • Articles of organization and operating agreement. We file the articles with the state and prepare the operating agreement that sets out how your company is owned and run.
  • EIN application for non-residents. We apply to the IRS for your Employer Identification Number, the US tax ID for your company. You don't need an SSN or ITIN.
  • Registered agent for 12 months. A registered agent in your state receives official mail and legal notices for your company.
  • Digital document pack. Your company documents, delivered digitally.

Which state?

Wyoming is our default: low yearly costs and strong privacy for owners. New Mexico has no annual report, so it has the lowest running cost. Delaware is best known with investors; it adds USD 100 to the package, and Delaware charges a USD 400 yearly tax from 2026. We help you choose in a free consultation.

What we need from you

  • A copy of your passport
  • Your full residential address and national ID number
  • A few company name choices
  • A short description of your business

You don't need to travel to the US, and non-residents can own 100% of a US LLC.

How long it takes

After our identity checks, the LLC is usually filed within 1 to 3 business days. The EIN usually follows 2 to 3 days after the company is incorporated. Government offices set their own pace, so these times are typical, not fixed.

What Starter does not include

  • A US business mailing address. It comes with the Business package (RM 7,899).
  • Bank and payment account applications. Business includes application support for one provider. You can also add an application per provider. Approval is always the bank's or provider's decision, based on their own checks.
  • Yearly filings. Every foreign-owned LLC must file Form 5472 each year. The first-year Form 5472 with pro-forma 1120 and the state annual report are in the Complete package (RM 12,099).

After the first year, renewal starts from USD 499 a year for the registered agent and the annual report.

Is Starter enough for you?

Starter suits founders who want the company and EIN first and will arrange the rest step by step. If you plan to sell on Amazon or Shopify soon, or you want help with payment applications from day one, the Business package is usually a better fit. If you want the first year's federal and state filings handled too, choose Complete.

Two things a US company does not do: it does not give you a visa, residency or the right to work in the US, and it does not decide your taxes on its own. Your tax position depends on your activity and where you live, so ask a qualified tax professional about your own case.
