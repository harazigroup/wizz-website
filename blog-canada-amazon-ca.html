Canada corporation for Amazon.ca sellers: Ontario or BC?

18 Nov 2026 · 3 min read

Selling on Amazon.ca from outside Canada? An Ontario or British Columbia corporation lets you form a Canadian company without a Canadian director. Here is how to choose between the two provinces and what to prepare.

Canada is a natural next market for many Amazon sellers, especially those who already sell in the US. If you want a Canadian company behind your Amazon.ca account, the first decision is where to incorporate: federally, or in one of the provinces.

Federal or provincial?

A Canadian corporation can be formed federally or in a province. A federal corporation needs at least 25% of its directors to be Canadian residents. Ontario and British Columbia (BC) have no resident-director rule, so a founder who lives outside Canada can be the only director.

That is why most international sellers form their corporation in one of these two provinces. A federal corporation is still available on request.

Ontario or BC: how to choose

For most Amazon.ca sellers, both provinces work well. The choice usually comes down to practical points:

  • Ontario: home to Toronto, Canada's largest city and business centre. A common choice if your partners, customers or service providers are in Eastern Canada.
  • British Columbia: home to Vancouver, on the Pacific coast. It can suit sellers whose suppliers are in Asia or who focus on Western Canada.
  • Sales tax: each province has its own sales tax rules. Ask a qualified tax professional how they apply to your products and your customers.

What forming the corporation involves

  1. Name search: a NUANS name search checks that your company name is available.
  2. Articles and bylaws: the corporation is registered with its articles, and the bylaws set out how it is run.
  3. Business Number: the Canada Revenue Agency issues a Business Number (BN), which identifies your company for tax purposes.
  4. Minute book: your corporate records, kept as a digital minute book.

Getting ready for Amazon.ca

Amazon checks that the details on your seller account match your documents. Before you apply, have these ready:

  • Your corporation's documents and Business Number
  • A registered office address in the province
  • GST/HST registration, if it applies to your business
  • Your passport and your full residential address
  • A business account in the company's name that can receive payouts

Decide early whether you sell your own brand or resell other brands, because it changes what Amazon may ask for. Amazon decides every seller account application, and banks and payment providers make their own decisions on business accounts.

A Canadian company is not a visa

Forming a corporation in Canada does not give you a visa, a work permit or residency. Immigration is a separate process, decided by the Canadian authorities.
