Thailand work permit through our Thai company: how it works

28 Oct 2026 · 3 min read

You can work in Thailand without first setting up your own Thai company. Here's how a work permit sponsored by our Thai company works, what it costs, and what the Thai authorities decide.

Many founders and professionals want to spend time in Thailand and work there legally. The usual assumption is that you must set up your own Thai company first. That route exists, but it isn't the only one, and for many people it isn't the simplest.

Our Thailand work permit service takes a different approach. You are employed under our Thai company, and our company sponsors your work permit application. This guide explains how that works and where the limits are.

Two ways to get a Thai work permit

  • Through your own Thai company. You form a Thai limited company and apply for a work permit through it. Each foreign work permit generally needs THB 2 million in paid-up capital and four Thai employees. Companies with BOI promotion follow different rules.
  • Through our Thai company. You are employed under our Thai company, which sponsors the application. You don't need your own Thai company, and you don't need to put up its capital.

How the sponsored route works

  1. Eligibility check. Before anything is submitted, we check whether your profile fits the requirements.
  2. Application. We prepare the work permit application and the supporting company documents.
  3. Visa coordination. We coordinate the matching visa alongside the work permit.
  4. Decision. The Thai authorities review the application and make the decision.

What the package includes

The Thailand work permit package costs RM 18,000 and covers two years:

  • Work permit and visa for 2 years
  • Sponsorship by our Thai company, so you don't need your own Thai company or capital
  • An eligibility check first
  • Application and visa coordination

What the work permit does and doesn't do

  • It lets you work only in the role and for the employer named on it. On this route, the employer is our Thai company.
  • It is a work permit and visa for a fixed term. It is not permanent residency.
  • The Thai authorities decide every application. We prepare it carefully, but no service can guarantee approval.
  • Forming a company, in Thailand or anywhere else, does not by itself give you a visa or residency.

When your own Thai company makes more sense

If you want to trade in Thailand through your own business, for example selling on Thai marketplaces under your own company, a Thai limited company may be the better fit. Keep in mind that foreign ownership is restricted for many activities. Our Thai company setup starts from RM 6,199 with government registration fees included. BOI promotion and a Foreign Business Licence are quoted separately.

For questions about tax on income you earn in Thailand, speak to a qualified tax adviser.
