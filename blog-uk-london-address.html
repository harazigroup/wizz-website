UK London address plans: registered office, director's address, business address and call answering

4 Nov 2026 · 3 min read

Every UK company needs a UK address, but founders abroad often need more than one kind. Here's what each London address service does, what our five plans cost, and how to choose.

When you form a UK private limited company from abroad, one of the first practical questions is which address to use. Companies House requires a UK registered office, and the addresses you give end up on the public register. Many founders also want a London address for customers, and someone to answer the phone in the company's name.

We offer five London address plans, each for 12 months, with prices set in pounds sterling. This guide explains the four services behind them so you can pick the right one.

1. Registered office address

Every UK company must have a registered office: a UK address where official letters, for example from Companies House and HMRC, can be delivered. It is shown publicly on the register. If you live outside the UK, you can't use your home address for this, so you need a UK address service.

Our UK Starter package already includes a registered office address for 12 months.

2. Director's service address

Every director gives Companies House a service address. This is the address shown on the public register for that director. Your home address is still given to Companies House, but it is not the one people see. A service address in London means your personal address doesn't need to appear as your contact address.

Our UK Business package includes a director's service address for 12 months.

3. Business address with mail handling

The registered office is for official mail. A business address is the one you put on your website, invoices and marketplace profiles, so customers and suppliers can write to you. Our virtual business address in London comes with mail handling.

4. Call answering

With call answering, calls to your UK business are answered in your company's name. It suits founders who sell to UK clients and want a professional first contact while they work from another time zone.

The five London address plans

  • Business address + registered office: £94.49
  • Business address + registered office + director's address: £100.09 (our most popular plan)
  • Business address + call answering: £220.50

How to choose

  • You only need what the law requires. Registered office + director's address covers the two addresses every UK company and director must give.
  • You sell online or invoice UK customers. Add a business address, so your public-facing address is in London too.
  • Clients call you. Look at business address + call answering, or the all-in-one London office.
  • You aren't sure. Tell us how the company will trade, and we'll suggest a plan before you pay.

Points to know before you start

  • Identity checks first. Under UK anti-money-laundering rules, address services must check the identity of the people behind the company before they start. We tell you exactly what to send.
  • An address is not a visa. A London address, like a UK company, does not give you a visa, residence or the right to work in the UK.
  • Banks and platforms decide for themselves. A London address can make your company details look complete, but banks, payment providers and marketplaces make their own decisions after their own checks.
