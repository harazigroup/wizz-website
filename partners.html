Partner questions

Who can become a partner?

Registered offices that serve business clients: business-service and PRO offices, consultants, accountants, law and travel offices. We open accounts after KYC.

Can I see the prices before applying?

Partner prices are shown in the portal after KYC. If you need an idea first, message us on WhatsApp.

How do I pay?

Each order gets an invoice. Pay by bank transfer or USDT (TRC-20). We start the work once payment and the client's documents are received.

Do you guarantee bank accounts or approvals?

No. Banks, payment providers, marketplaces and governments make their own decisions. We prepare complete applications. Please don't promise approvals to your clients.
