EIN for non-residents: what it is and how the application works

11 Nov 2026 · 3 min read

Your US LLC needs its own US tax ID, the EIN. You can get one without an SSN or ITIN and without travelling to the US. Here is what it is, what it is used for and how the application works.

Forming a US LLC gives you a company. To use it for banking, selling and yearly filings, the company also needs a US tax ID: the EIN, or Employer Identification Number. It is issued by the IRS, the US federal tax authority.

Many non-US founders think they need a US social security number or a US address of their own to get one. They don't.

What an EIN is

An EIN is the number that identifies your company to the IRS. It belongs to the company, not to you personally. Despite the name, you don't need employees to have one. Almost every US LLC gets an EIN soon after it is formed.

What you use it for

  • Bank and payment applications. Providers such as Wise, Payoneer and Stripe ask for your company's EIN. Each provider still makes its own decision after its own checks.
  • Marketplace seller accounts. Selling on Amazon.com, Walmart or TikTok Shop US with a US company means giving the platform your EIN. Approval is the platform's decision.
  • Yearly federal filings. A foreign-owned single-member LLC files Form 5472 with a pro-forma Form 1120 each year, under its EIN.
  • Invoices and contracts. US clients and partners often ask for your company's tax ID.

No SSN or ITIN needed

A non-resident owner can get an EIN for their LLC without a social security number (SSN) or an individual taxpayer number (ITIN). You also don't need to travel to the US. The whole application can be done remotely.

How the application works

  1. Your LLC is formed first. The IRS issues an EIN to a company that exists, so we file your LLC with the state first. This usually takes 1 to 3 business days after our identity checks.
  2. We prepare and submit the application. We apply to the IRS for your company's EIN using the company details and the owner details you gave us.
  3. You receive the EIN confirmation letter. The EIN usually follows 2 to 3 days after the company is incorporated. The IRS sets its own pace, so this time is typical, not fixed.

What we need from you

A copy of your passport, your full residential address, your national ID number, a few company name choices and a short description of your business. Please give your full home address, as it appears on your official documents.

Changes later on

If you add a partner to your LLC later, the company's tax setup changes and a new EIN may be needed. We coordinate this together with the amended operating agreement. Your personal tax is a separate question: it depends on where you live and how you run the business, so speak to a qualified tax professional in your country.
