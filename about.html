About us

A Malaysian company built for founders who work across borders

Based in Kuala Lumpur, working with clients in Arabic and English.

Wizz Smart Services Sdn. Bhd. is a business services company registered in Malaysia and based in Kuala Lumpur. We help entrepreneurs and companies from the Gulf, the Middle East, Africa and Asia build a legal, working presence in 12 countries across North America, Europe, the Middle East and Asia.

Most of our clients need more than a registration certificate. They need a company they can actually trade with: a structure that fits their activity, the right documents, a realistic plan for banking and payments, and a way to start selling. We bring those pieces together and stay with the client after formation.

We handle most services directly ourselves. Where the law requires a licensed local party, such as a registered agent in US states, we work with trusted registered providers. In Malaysia we partner with a registered company secretary office in Kuala Lumpur. Either way, you deal with one team.

Our promise

Clear about what we do, and what we can't promise

Some approvals belong to banks, platforms and governments. We tell you that up front so you can plan with real expectations.

  • A written quotation before you pay
  • Advice matched to your activity and residence
  • Complete, carefully prepared applications

What no honest provider can guarantee

  • Bank account or Stripe, Wise or Payoneer approval
