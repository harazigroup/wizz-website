New · From Malaysia

Amazon FBA preparation from Malaysia

Source or send your products to us in Malaysia. We check, label and pack them to Amazon's requirements and ship them to Amazon fulfilment centres in the US, UK, Canada and Europe.

Shipments must meet Amazon's prep, labelling and product rules. Amazon makes the final decision on receiving inventory.

  1. ReceiveProducts from your supplier, or sourced by us in Malaysia
  2. Inspect and countQuantity and visible condition checked against your order
  3. Label and packFNSKU labels, bagging, bundling and carton labels
  4. Ship to AmazonAir or sea freight to the fulfilment centre Amazon assigns
  5. TrackUpdates until the shipment is checked in
