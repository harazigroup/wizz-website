Marketplace onboarding from RM 300 per platform: what we prepare

27 Oct 2026 · 3 min read

A seller application is quick to start and easy to get wrong. Here's what our marketplace onboarding covers, what you send us, and what the platform decides.

Opening a seller account on Noon, TikTok Shop, Shopee or Lazada looks simple: fill in a form, upload a few documents, wait. In practice, applications often stall over small things. A company name written two different ways. An address that doesn't match the registration documents. A bank account in another name. Or a country the platform doesn't accept for your company.

Our marketplace onboarding service is there to get those details right before you submit. It costs RM 300 per platform and can be added to any of our company packages.

Which platforms it covers

The service covers Noon, TikTok Shop, Shopee, Lazada and other marketplaces, from LINE in Thailand to Cdiscount in France and eMAG in Central and Eastern Europe. You can see the full list on our marketplaces page. Amazon has its own service: Amazon seller account application support is RM 1,999.

What we prepare

  • A fit check. We confirm that the platform accepts your company in the market you want to sell in. TikTok Shop, for example, sets its own rules in each country.
  • Your document set. We gather the documents the platform asks for, such as your company registration documents, passport and proof of address.
  • Consistent details. We check that the company name, address, owner details and payout account match your company documents everywhere they appear.
  • A complete application. We prepare and submit a complete application, so nothing is left blank or out of date.

What you send us

After you pay, you fill in a short onboarding form and send your details and documents, so we can check eligibility. We confirm everything with you before anything is submitted. If you don't have a company yet, we can form one first in one of the 14 countries we serve.

Own brand or reseller?

Before you apply, be clear about what you sell: your own brand, or products from brands that belong to others. The answer changes which documents each platform asks for. Our guide own brand or reseller? explains the difference.

What to keep in mind

  • Each marketplace approves its own seller accounts. We make sure your application is complete and consistent, but no service can guarantee approval.
  • Platform rules and accepted countries change. We check the current requirements when we prepare your application.
  • Payouts need a bank or payment account in your company's name. Each bank or provider decides on its own applications.
  • Forming a company abroad does not give you a visa or residency. For tax questions about selling in another country, speak to a qualified tax adviser.
