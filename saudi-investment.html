Questions

Saudi investment questions

Does an established company guarantee my Saudi investment licence?

No. It can support your application, but the Saudi authorities decide every licence, and their requirements can change. We check your plan with you before you choose a company.

Which country should I choose?

It depends on your nationality, your plan and the documents your Saudi application needs. Tell us your plan on WhatsApp and we'll recommend one.

What do you check before the transfer?

The company's legal status, its filings and its documents. If anything isn't right, we propose another company.

What do I need to provide?

For Malaysia, the UK and Egypt, a passport copy only. For Pakistan, non-Pakistanis need a passport and an attested power of attorney; Pakistanis need their national ID card and the mobile number registered to it, for OTP verification.

How long does it take?

Malaysia and the UK take 2 to 3 weeks, Egypt 3 to 4 weeks and Pakistan about 6 weeks. Government processing and public holidays can add time.

Can I buy online?

No. Each company is different, so we talk first, match you with an available company, then send an invoice.

Do you help with the Saudi company itself?

Yes. Our Saudi company setup, with the investment licence application, starts from RM 12,000, quoted after a short call. Government fees and capital are not included.
