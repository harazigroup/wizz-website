What we do

Three services, one brand file

Trademark filing

We file your trademark with the right office, for example the USPTO in the US or the UKIPO in the UK. Amazon accepts a pending application, so you don't have to wait for registration to apply.

Amazon Brand Registry

We prepare and submit your enrolment: the brand name exactly as on the trademark, your logo, product categories and photos of the brand on your product or packaging.

Amazon Vendor Central

Vendor Central is by Amazon's invitation. We help you build the brand, listings and sales record Amazon looks for, and support your onboarding if you're invited.

Seller Central or Vendor Central

Two ways to sell on Amazon

Seller Central

You sell to customers as a third-party seller.

  • You set your own prices
  • You keep control of your listings and stock
  • Open to any brand that registers

Vendor Central

Amazon buys your stock wholesale and sells it to customers.

  • Amazon sets the retail price
  • Payment on Amazon's terms, often 30 to 90 days
  • By Amazon's invitation only

What you need for Brand Registry

Amazon checks these against your trademark record.

  • A registered or pending trademark (a word mark, or a logo with words)
  • Your brand name exactly as on the trademark
  • Photos of the brand permanently on your product or packaging, not stickers
  • Your product categories

Questions

Amazon brand questions

Do I need a registered trademark first?

No. Amazon accepts a pending trademark application from an approved trademark office, so you can apply once it's filed.

Can I enrol a brand I resell?

No. Only the trademark owner can enrol a brand. If you resell other brands, Brand Registry isn't for you, but we can help you set up as a seller.

Can you get me into Vendor Central?

Vendor Central is by Amazon's invitation, so no one can guarantee access. We help you build the sales record and brand presence Amazon looks for.

Does Amazon charge for Brand Registry?

No. Amazon doesn't charge to enrol. Our fee and the trademark office fees are in your written quote.

Which country should I file my trademark in?

Usually where you sell first. Most of our clients start with the US or the UK. Tell us your markets and we'll recommend the office.
