FAQ

Common questions

Didn't find your answer? Send us your question using the form above, or chat with us on WhatsApp.

Do I need to live in the USA or UK to own a company there?

No. Non-residents can own US and UK companies. Some steps, such as banking and tax registration, have extra requirements for non-residents, and we explain them during the consultation.

Will a company abroad give me a visa or residency?

No. Forming a company is separate from immigration. A company does not by itself give you a visa or the right to live in that country. In some countries, such as Thailand, a company that meets the official requirements can later support a work permit and visa application, but the authorities make that decision.

Can you guarantee a Stripe or bank account?

No one can. Each provider decides based on its own eligibility, KYC and risk checks. We help you choose suitable providers and prepare a complete application.

I want to sell on Amazon. What should I tell you first?

Tell us whether you sell your own private brand or resell other brands, which marketplace and country you are targeting, and where you live. The right structure depends on these answers.

How long does it take, and how much does it cost?

It depends on the jurisdiction and the services you need. After a short consultation we send a written quotation with fees and expected timelines.
